Section 09

Lead (Sisa) Awareness Awareness survey

The awareness cascade the campaign is trying to move: knowing what lead is → knowing it reaches turmeric → being concerned about children → knowing what to do about it → actually doing it. Each step loses people, and the size of each drop is where the communication strategy earns or loses its money.
